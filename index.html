02Who I am

A curious mind that likes to lead

I'm Steven Widinata, a Master of Information Technology (MIT) student at BINUS University. I keep asking "how does this work?" and then I go and build it.

At school I organised tournaments and led a volleyball committee. At university I learn competitive programming with Jollybee, build web apps with React and Node in Ureeka, guide new students as a Freshman Leader & Partner, and mentor them academically at SASC.

To me, every new skill is a star. Connect enough of them and you start to see the shape of what's possible.
